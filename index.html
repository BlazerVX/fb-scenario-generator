🎄 Silent Night, Stolen Gifts  · Christmas · A Fan-made Community Project · draft v2
Snow lies thick on the field and the gifts lie scattered across it, nine piles of them, wrapped and ribboned and utterly unguarded. Every Warband has come with a Wish List and every Warband means to fill it. Somewhere among the piles, small and green and spiteful, Seu-Grin is already at work.

A Fan-made Community Project by BlazerVX. Unofficial. Not a Para Bellum product. It uses Para Bellum's Festive Loot Gnome scenario for Conquest: The Last Argument of Kings (TLAOK) as its base, with characters adapted from Festive and Mean, reworked for First Blood.

How many Players?

2 Players, 1,000 points each, on a 4'×4' Battlefield with wintry Terrain.

Setting up the Board

Gift Piles: divide the Battlefield into a 4×4 grid of 12" squares. Place a Gift Pile on each of the nine points where the inner grid lines cross (12", 24" and 36" from the table edges). Each Gift Pile is the center of a 6" Critical Site. Gift Piles are decorative: like any Critical Site they are ignored for Movement and Line of Sight. If the boxes get in the way of a model, nudge the boxes.

Seu-Grin is Placed in contact with the central Gift Pile.

Each circle is a 6" Critical Site. Grid squares are 12".

Each Player's Deployment Zone is the 12"×12" corner to the right of their own table edge, so the two zones sit in opposite corners.

Territory

Deployment: roll for Priority as normal (RB p.48). The First Player chooses a Deployment Zone and the first Tactic. Players then alternate Placing Command Cards wholly within their Deployment Zone, further than 9" from any Enemy Command Cards, then alternate Revealing them as normal.

Tactics

Starting with the First Player, each Player chooses one Tactic. Each Player must choose a different one.

  • Sleigh Bells: during the first Round, you are automatically the Supremacy Player.
  • Stocking Stuffer: after Deployment, place one of your gift tags on any Gift Pile.
  • Checking It Twice: once per Battle, during a Victory Phase before declaring, move one of your gift tags to a different Gift Pile (one that does not already hold one of your tags).

Scenario Rules

Wish Lists: before Deployment, each Player secretly draws one of the six Wish Lists at random. The two Players must draw different lists. Keep yours hidden until you declare or the Battle ends.

Each Wish List is drawn as you see the table from your own table edge: the top row is furthest from you. Your Deployment Zone is the shaded corner at the bottom right, the Enemy's is at the top left. Every list holds exactly 5 Gift Piles (large red dots).

Gift Tags: each Player needs about 9 gift tags in their own colour. In each Victory Phase, place one of your tags on every Gift Pile you are Seizing (DS p.7: non-Broken Warriors at least partially within contribute, Warriors on a Vantage Point never do, the higher total of remaining Wounds Seizes it, otherwise it is Contested). Contested Piles gain no tags. A Pile can hold both Players' tags, but never more than one tag per Player. Tags stay until removed.

Untag: (Out of Combat Action) Remove one of your own gift tags from a Gift Pile this Warrior is at least partially within.

Supremacy Ties: this Scenario has no Victory Points. If the Supremacy Roll is a draw, the Player with fewer gift tags on the Battlefield chooses who will be the Supremacy Player. If still tied, Re-Roll.

Who Invited Him?: Seu-Grin belongs to neither Warband. Each Round the Supremacy Player controls him: he is Friendly to their Warband for now, they take his Command Card, Activate him as one of their own Warriors and perform his Command Ability. He never contributes to Seizing a Gift Pile, and never benefits from or grants the controlling Warband's Army Rules or Draw Events (such as Exertion). When control of Seu-Grin changes, he and any Warriors that are now Friendly to him are no longer Engaged; nobody is Driven Back.

Christmas Miracle: the Player whose Warrior Slays Seu-Grin may immediately place one of their gift tags on any Gift Pile, or remove one of their own tags from any Gift Pile.

Return of the Grinch: if Seu-Grin is Slain, the next time a Player becomes the Supremacy Player, they immediately Place him anywhere further than 6" from all Warriors. He Activates as normal.

Mission: Victory Phase

After removing Command Cards with no Warriors left, Players place gift tags on the Gift Piles they are Seizing. From the end of the third Round onward, each Player may then declare their Wish List filled. Both decide at the same time (for example, by revealing a closed fist that does or does not hold a gift tag).

  • Tags on exactly your Wish List's Gift Piles, no more and no less: you win the Battle!
  • Wrong: reveal nothing, remove 3 of your own tags of your choice (or all of them if you have fewer), and play on.
  • Both declare correctly in the same Victory Phase: Seu-Grin steals Christmas and both Players lose!

Mission: End of Battle

If no one has won after 6 Rounds, both Players reveal their Wish Lists. Score +1 for each of your tags on a Pile from your list and −1 for each of your tags on any other Pile. Highest score wins. If the scores are tied, Seu-Grin wins and everybody gets coal.

🎃 Fright Night  · Halloween · Para Bellum
Fishidious has drenched the land in dour darkness. Fight by the full moon and use your lantern to rally wary warriors and ward off the wicked!

How many Players?

2 Players or Teams, about 1,000 points per side (2 players 1,000 each; 4 players 500; 6 players 333). 4'×4' Battlefield with spooky Terrain. Fishidious is Placed as close to the center as possible.

Territory

Roll off, then alternate clockwise Placing Command Cards anywhere in your Territory further than 9" from Fishidious and any Enemy Command Cards. Reveal as normal. Then each Player chooses one Friendly Warrior to carry a Lantern.

Scenario Rules

Insidious Intent: each Round the Supremacy Player controls Fishidious, takes his Command Card and Activates him as one of their own.

Foul Fishy Phylactery: if Fishidious is Slain, the next Player to win Supremacy Places him anywhere outside all Enemy Command Ranges.

Lantern's Light: Warriors within 3" of a Lantern or its carrier are Illuminated and Re-Roll failed Resolve Tests.

Lanterns: a 1" marker, ignored for Movement and Line of Sight. A Warrior Placed on it or ending a Move in contact with it Seizes it and carries it until Slain or removed; it then drops under the base (or as close as possible if on a Vantage Point). One Lantern per Warrior.

Darkest Night: Warriors cannot draw Line of Sight beyond their Command Range unless the Target is Illuminated. Warriors that are not Illuminated must Re-Roll successful Resolve Tests of 1.

Teams (more than 2 players): all Players roll for Supremacy by team. Each team turn, one Player activates one Command Card; the same Player cannot activate in two consecutive team turns unless no other cards remain. Teams share a VP pool, and teammates' Warriors are neither Friendly nor Enemy (but are affected by rules that affect all Warriors).

Mission: Victory Phase

  • 3 VP to whoever Slays Fishidious.
  • 2 VP for carrying the most Lanterns.
  • 1 VP for having fewer Broken! Warriors on the Battlefield.

Mission: End of Battle (after Round 6)

  • 3 VP for the Warrior closest to the center.
  • 2 VP for more surviving [Characters] (Fishidious doesn't count).
  • 1 VP per Lantern carried.
